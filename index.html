WHY SPARQIQ

More than a box of parts

Many starter kits stop at "copy the circuit, upload the code". SparqIQ teaches the four skills real engineers use.

Wiring

Students wire every circuit themselves from a breadboard photo, diagram and pin table.

Coding

Live demos light up each line of code as it runs, then challenges ask students to change it.

Troubleshooting

Every project teaches how to find faults: observe, guess, test one thing at a time.

Real products

A reusable laser-cut MDF set turns circuits into working models students are proud of.
